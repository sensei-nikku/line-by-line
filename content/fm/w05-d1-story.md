## Track 0: Previously, Jordan

At 4:51 on Friday, Jordan texted Brooke. @Jordan"I'm not signing. Thank you for holding it. Really."

Then he called his dad. He didn't ask for money. He asked for advice.

His dad didn't have much to give anymore, but he did have some news. An old friend of his, Ray Pellegrino, ran operations for NEJC's whole region. Ray had said the Phoenix warehouse was about to lose its assistant manager. @Dad"I can't get you that job," his dad said. @Dad"Nobody can. But now you know it's open."

That part was luck. Jordan applied that night. So did forty other people.

Ray didn't start with the résumés. On Monday, he walked the dock and asked the people who would know.

Marisol didn't even look up from her scanner. @Marisol"He stays late to fix his mistakes," she said. @Marisol"Then he stays late to fix mine."

Julioberto said, @Julioberto"When a temp got shorted on her check, he walked her to HR and stood there until she got paid."

Yvette said, @Yvette"I've been here nineteen years. He's the first new kid who learned everybody's name in a week." Then she signed the reference form twice by mistake.

Later, Ray told Jordan, @Ray"Your dad got you a heads-up. Your crew got you the job."

The raise was the biggest number Jordan had ever seen on a paycheck. He did what Marisol taught him. He wrote down the whole number before he spent any of it. He remembered what he'd learned. Mostly.

He found a one-bedroom near the light rail on 19th Avenue. It cost over five hundred dollars a month less than the place on Roosevelt Row, and it didn't have a rooftop pool. He took the train to work. He put the credit card on the fridge instead of in his wallet, and he left it there until it was paid off.

He signed up for a cooking class at the community college. @Jordan"I'm done paying strangers to feed me," he told Marisol. He got good fast. He got so good that the diner by his train stop hired him for Saturday mornings, six to eleven, at twenty dollars an hour. He saved every dollar from that job for one thing.

Every Friday, eleven breakfast burritos still showed up on the dock table. Chorizo for Julioberto. Bean and cheese for Marisol.

@Marisol"You don't have to do that anymore," Marisol told him.

@Jordan"I know," Jordan said. @Jordan"That's why I work Saturdays."
